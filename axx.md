# **XD&X: a 24XX Refit For 1st Edition **

### **RULES**

#### **Ability Modifiers**

From your **3d6 Ability Scores (3–18)**:

\

- **3:** -3 \| **4–5:** -2 \| **6–8:** -1
  \
- **9–12:** 0
  \
- **13–15:** +1 \| **16–17:** +2 \| **18:** +3
  \

#### **Core Checks & Saving Throws**

- **Attribute Checks:** Roll **1d20 + Ability Mod** vs **TN 12**.
  \
- **Saving Throws (Roll-Under):** When exposed to a hazard, roll **1d20**. If you roll **equal to or under** your class Target Number for that hazard type, you succeed.
  \
  - *Level Progression:* Reduce all Save Target Numbers by **1 per Level** (minimum 2).
    \

| **Hazard Type** | **Fighter Target** | **Cleric Target** | **Magic-User Target** | **Thief Target** |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| **1. Poison / Death** | **12** | 10 | 14 | 13 |
| **2. Wands / Staves** | 14 | 12 | **11** | 14 |
| **3. Petrification / Polymorph** | 15 | 13 | 13 | 12 |
| **4. Breath Weapon** | 17 | 16 | 15 | **13** |
| **5. Spells** | 16 | **15** | **12** | 15 |

#### **Turns, Time & Resources**

- **Dungeon Turn (10 min):** Collective party action (explore, search, disarm trap, rest).
  \
  - *Torches:* 1 torch lasts **6 Dungeon Turns** (1 hour).
    \
  - *Rations:* Consume 1 daily ration per **Long Rest** (6 hours) or take -2 to all rolls.
    \
- **Combat Round (1 min):** High side on **1d6 Initiative** acts first. Move + 1 action (Attack, Cast, Use Item).
  \
  - *Attack:* Roll **1d20 + Attack Mod** vs **Target AC** (Target = 20 - Enemy AC).
    \

#### **Healing & Death**

- **Short Rest (1 turn):** Bind wounds once/day to regain **1d4 HP**.
  \
- **Spells & Potions:** *Cure Light Wounds* or Healing Potions heal **1d8 HP** instantly.
  \
- **0 HP & Recovery:** At 0 HP, roll **1d6 Injury & Death**. If killed or retired, a replacement character enters immediately at the next room or scene.
  \

#### **Treasure, XP & Leveling**

- **Looting:** Roll **Treasure Table** when searching rooms, chests, or boss enemies.
  \
- **1 GP = 1 XP** (brought safely to town).
  \
  - **Lvl 2:** 2,000 XP \| **Lvl 3:** 4,000 XP \| **Lvl 4:** 8,000 XP
    \
  - *Level Up:* **+1 Hit Die (d6 HP)**, **+1 Attack Mod**, **-1 to all Save Targets**.
    \

### **CLASSES, SPELLS & EQUIPMENT**

#### **Classes**

- **Fighter:** d8 HP/lvl. +2 Attack. Uses any weapon/armor. Best vs **Poison / Death**.
  \
- **Cleric:** d6 HP/lvl. +1 Attack. Blunt weapons, medium/heavy armor. Best vs **Spells / Magic**. *Turn Undead:* Roll **1d20 + WIS vs TN 12** to turn d8 HD of undead.
  \
- **Magic-User:** d4 HP/lvl. +0 Attack. Dagger/Staff, no armor. Best vs **Wands & Spells**.
  \
- **Thief:** d6 HP/lvl. +1 Attack. Light armor. Best vs **Breath Weapons**. +2 stealth/locks/traps. *Backstab:* +4 attack & 2x damage against unaware targets.
  \

#### **Spells & Magic**

Prepare **1 spell per level per day** (6-hour rest required). Casting takes 1 turn action; taking damage before your turn breaks the spell.

\

- **Cleric Spells:** *Cure Light Wounds* (1d8 HP), *Light* (30ft radius, 1 hr), *Bless* (+1 party attacks for 1 fight).
  \
- **Magic-User Spells:** *Magic Missile* (1d4+1 auto-hit damage), *Sleep* (puts 2d4 HD of weak foes to sleep), *Shield* (+4 AC for 1 fight), *Invisibility* (invisible until attacking/casting).
  \

#### **Gear List (Start with 3d6 x 10 GP)**

- **Weapons:** Light (1d4 dmg, 1 slot) — **2 GP** \| Medium (1d6 dmg, 1 slot) — **10 GP** \| Heavy (1d10 dmg, 2 slots) — **15 GP**.
  \
- **Armor:** Light (AC 7) — **20 GP** \| Medium (AC 5, 2 slots) — **75 GP** \| Heavy (AC 3, 2 slots) — **100 GP** \| Shield (+1 AC, 1 slot) — **10 GP**.
  \
- **Supplies:** Rations (7 days, 1 slot) — **5 GP** \| Torches (6, 1 slot) — **1 GP** \| Rope 50ft — **1 GP** \| Thieves' Tools — **30 GP** \| Holy Symbol — **25 GP**.
  \

### **TABLES & GENERATORS**

#### **1d8 Threat / Encounter**

*(Monster stats: **HD** = Hit Dice/Level, **AC** = Descending Armor Class, **Atk** = Attack Mod/Damage)*

\

1.  **Goblin Band:** HD 1, HP 4, AC 7, Atk +0 (1d6). 1d6 goblins ambush.
    \
2.  **Orc Patrol:** HD 2, HP 9, AC 6, Atk +1 (1d8). 1d4 brutal warriors demanding blood or toll.
    \
3.  **Skeleton Guard:** HD 1, HP 5, AC 7, Atk +0 (1d6). Mindless, immune to sleep/poison.
    \
4.  **Ghoul:** HD 2, HP 10, AC 6, Atk +1 (1d3 + Paralysis: Roll **Save vs Paralyzation** or immobilized 1d4 turns).
    \
5.  **Rival Adventurers:** Equal count to party. HD = Party Avg Level. Mixed classes.
    \
6.  **Dark Cultist:** HD 3, HP 12, AC 8, Atk +1 (1d4 + Casts *Cause Light Wounds* for 1d8 dmg).
    \
7.  **Cursed Trap:** Roll **Save vs Poison/Traps**. Takes 2d6 damage or venom on failure.
    \
8.  **Ogre:** HD 4, HP 19, AC 5, Atk +4 (2d6 heavy club).
    \

#### **1d4 Location & Environment**

1.  **Ruined Keep:** Collapse-prone stone, moldering tapestries, echoing footfalls.
    \
2.  **Sunken Crypt:** Stagnant knee-deep water, damp granite, ancient rot smell.
    \
3.  **Fungal Caverns:** Bioluminescent spores, slippery terrain, choking haze.
    \
4.  **Defiled Temple:** Shattered marble altars, blood-stained pillars, ominous hum.
    \

#### **1d8 Treasure & Loot**

1.  **Loose Pouch:** 2d6 x 10 GP.
    \
2.  **Chest:** 1d6 x 100 GP + 1d4 Gemstones (50 GP each).
    \
3.  **Ornate Urn:** Silverware and art objects worth 300 GP.
    \
4.  **Potion:** Healing (restores 1d8 HP) or Invisibility (1 hour).
    \
5.  **Scroll:** Contains 1 random Magic-User or Cleric spell (single-use cast).
    \
6.  **Magic Weapon:** +1 to Attack and Damage rolls.
    \
7.  **Magic Armor:** +1 AC improvement over base type.
    \
8.  **Hoard:** 1d6 x 500 GP, 2 Gems, and 1 Magic Item (roll again 1–7).
    \

#### **1d6 Injury & Death (at 0 HP)**

1.  **Fatal:** Dead. Retire character immediately; replacement enters next turn.
    \
2.  **Maimed:** Lost limb or eye (-2 to physical rolls). Retire or play on.
    \
3.  **Grievous Wound:** Unconscious; out of action until healed or rested.
    \
4.  **Scarred:** Permanent facial scar (-1 CHA or +1 Intimidation).
    \
5.  **Shaken:** Disadvantage on next 3 rolls; drop held items.
    \
6.  **Barely Alive:** Regain 1 HP, but incapacitated for 1 turn.
