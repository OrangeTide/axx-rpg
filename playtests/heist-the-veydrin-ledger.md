# AX&X Solo Playtest: The Veydrin Ledger (a heist)

A solo, GM-and-players-both run of the **core rules only** (`src/axx.typ`), aimed
at a different shape of problem from the dungeon sessions: an **infiltration**.
The goal was to exercise the Thief's skills end to end (climb, stealth, listen,
locks, find and disarm trap, backstab) and to see whether the other classes have
real jobs when the work is a quiet break-in rather than a fight. Theme: break
into a nobleman's estate and steal a ledger that proves his treason. Every die
was a real shell roll, quoted inline as `[dice] = total`.

## The Job

A reeve of the Count hires the party for **300 GP**: recover the ledger Lord
Castellan Veydrin keeps in his study strongbox, the one that proves he has been
selling the marches' grain to the orc marches and pocketing the levy. The brief
adds a condition that shapes every choice: **no bodies**. A dead household brings
investigators; a quiet theft the lord cannot even report brings none. Night
infiltration of the walled estate.

## The Party

Four characters, abilities rolled 3d6 in order, kindred and the Human +1 then
assigned to fit. Mod table: 3 = -3, 4-5 = -2, 6-8 = -1, 9-12 = 0, 13-15 = +1,
16-17 = +2, 18 = +3. The rolls ran rough, a -1 INT Magic-User and a weak-STR
Fighter, and were kept as-is for an honest test.

### Pip, Halfling Thief (Neutral), the infiltrator

- Rolls: STR `[1 3 6]`=10 (0), DEX `[5 3 2]`=10 (0), CON `[5 5 4]`=14 (+1), INT `[4 5 2]`=11 (0), WIS `[6 6 5]`=17 (+2), CHA `[5 3 1]`=9 (-1).
- Halfling: reroll one failed d20 per day, +2 to stealth checks, +1 AC vs larger foes, no two-handed weapons.
- HP 7 (max d6 + CON +1). AC 11 (light). +2 Dodge. Dagger 1d4, Backstab +4 and double damage on the unaware.
- Stealth, locks, traps, climbing are Class Checks at 1d20 + Level 1 + DEX 0 + 2 = **1d20+3**; stealth is **1d20+5** with the Halfling bonus.
- WIS +2 gives strong perception and a good Body/Will. DEX 0 is a soft spot for a Thief, carried by the +Level and the class +2.

### Mireth, Elf Magic-User (Neutral), the opener

- Rolls: STR `[2 4 1]`=7 (-1), DEX `[4 4 4]`=12 (0), CON `[1 1 5]`=7 (-1), INT `[3 2 1]`=6 (-1), WIS `[6 1 2]`=9 (0), CHA `[6 5 2]`=13 (+1).
- Elf: immune to Sleep and Charmed, sees 60 ft in the dark.
- HP **3** (max d4 + CON -1). AC 10. **1 slot** (INT -1 grants no bonus slot). Book: _Sleep_, _Charm Person_; prepared _Sleep_.
- Darkvision let her move lightless. The single slot and 3 HP are the story of her night: one decisive spell, then a fragile passenger.

### Brother Aldric, Human Cleric (Lawful), the face

- Rolls: STR `[4 5 6]`=15 (+1), DEX `[3 2 3]`=8 (-1), CON `[4 5 4]`=13 (+1), INT `[1 2 5]`=8 (-1), WIS `[4 3 4]`=11 (0), CHA `[3 6 6]`=15, Human +1 to CHA, 15 to **16 (+2)**.
- HP 7 (max d6 + CON +1). AC 14 (medium). +2 Will. Mace 1d6+1. **1 slot** (WIS 0, no bonus), prepared _Cure Light Wounds_. Can Turn Undead.
- CHA 16 made him the social lever. His medium armor made him the climbing liability.

### Garren, Human Fighter (Neutral), the muscle

- Rolls: STR `[3 1 1]`=5, Human +1 to **6 (-1)**, DEX `[4 5 4]`=13 (+1), CON `[6 2 1]`=9 (0), INT `[6 2 1]`=9 (0), WIS `[5 2 3]`=10 (0), CHA `[1 3 1]`=5 (-2).
- HP 8 (max d8 + CON 0). AC 14 (medium + shield). +2 Body.
- Melee +0 (Warrior +1, STR -1); missile +2 (Warrior +1, DEX +1). The weak STR blunts his sword and his hauling; his value is the second body in a two-on-one, and a bow if the range opens.

## Scene 1: The Spiked Wall

A twelve-foot estate wall, iron-spiked. Pip free-climbs it to fix the rope:
Class Check `[10]`+3 = **13 vs 12, up**. He sinks the grapnel and drops the line.
Garren, steadying a loose spike to quiet it, rolls a **natural 1** `[2]`-1: the
spike shears off and clatters down the stone. The patrol-timing die lands `[1]`
at the worst moment, two men-at-arms (HD 1, HP 6, AC 13) round the corner
lantern-first.

Mireth spends her one spell: **_Sleep_, 2d4** `[2 2]` = 4 HD affected, no save,
both HD-1 guards crumple in silence. Her entire magical allotment for the night
is gone in the first minute.

The others take the rope. Garren `[20]` and Mireth `[15]` go over cleanly.
**Aldric cannot make it**: `[4]`-1 = 3, then a second try with Pip's Aid `[5]`-1+2
= 6, both under the easy 9. Medium armor is dead weight on a rope. Rather than
burn more time, Pip drops down the inside and unbars the **servant gate**, and
Aldric simply walks in. The two sleeping guards meant no one was watching it.

The post-noise wandering re-roll comes up `[2]` (a 1 or 2 after noise brings a
wanderer): the kennel stirs, and a houndmaster crosses the far lawn with a
mastiff.

## Scene 2: The Lawn

Mireth is already spell-dry, so the dog is a legwork problem, not a magic one.
Pip leads the quiet route; a keen nose makes it hard (15+): stealth `[6]`+5 = 11,
a miss. He spends the **Halfling reroll**: `[12]`+5 = **17, past it**. The
mastiff never catches the scent, and he walks the crew along the hedge to the
gate he already opened.

## Scene 3: The Servant Door, Inside

Pip listens at the service passage, Class Check `[14]`+3 = 17, all quiet, then
picks the lock `[18]`+3 = **21, clean and silent**. The wandering die `[6]` is
empty. They are in the house.

## Scene 4: The Night Butler

An old half-blind butler shuffles the corridor with a candle. The Cleric's robes
are the cover: Aldric claims to be a priest summoned at this hour to pray over
the ailing lady of the house. CHA Check `[14]`+2 = **16 vs 12**. The butler bows
the holy man and his servants through and shuffles off. The single cleanest
obstacle bypass of the run, and one no other class could have sold.

## Scene 5: The Study Door

A trapped, locked door. Pip searches for the trap first, Class Check `[6]`+3 = 9,
**under 12, missed**. The poison needle in the lock-plate springs when he works
it. Body save `[17]`+1 = **18 vs 12**, the venom stings but does not take (CON
+1 and a good roll; the 1d6 `[3]` is not applied).

Then the dice turn cold on the Thief's core job. The study lock: `[3]`+3 = 6,
fail; retry `[5]`+3 = 8, fail; retry `[16]`+3 = **19, open** on the third pass.
Every retry is a 10-minute Turn, and the wandering die rolls each time (`[3]`,
safe).

## Scene 6: The Strongbox

Inside, an iron coffer holds the ledger. Pip's picks fail outright: `[3]`+3 = 6,
then `[2]`+3 = 5. Three straight failures on the box (counting the first attempt
earlier). The desk also holds decoy account-books; the best read on which ledger
is the true one is Pip's WIS perception `[12]`+2 = 14, one short of the hard 15,
so the party cannot be sure by sight. Low INT across the whole crew means no one
can verify it. Wandering `[4]`, safe, but they have burned the better part of an
hour in the house.

## The Climax and Escape

As Pip bends to the coffer, the study's inner door eases open: **Veydrin's
bodyguard** (HD 2, HP 10, AC 15, +2 / 1d8) on his rounds. The crew is hunched
over the box.

- **Surprise:** party `[2]` caught off guard; bodyguard `[4]` not. The guard
  gets the free round, and the Thief's would-be backstab (`[11]`+5 to hit, 2d4
  `[4 2]` = 6 doubled damage ready) **never fires**, denied by losing surprise.
- **Round 1 (surprise):** the guard bellows "THIEVES" and strikes Pip, `[17]`+2
  = 19 vs AC 11, `[3]` = 3 damage (Pip 7 to 4).
- **Round 2:** two-on-one (+2 each). Aldric's mace `[16]`+4 = hit, `[2]`+1 = 2;
  Garren's sword `[17]`+2 = hit, `[4]` = 4. Guard 10 to 4. Pip abandons the lock
  and **hefts the whole coffer**, STR check `[14]` = 14, routing around the pick
  he could not make. Reinforcements are `[2]` rounds out.
- **Round 3:** Garren pommels the guard down, `[18]`+2 = hit, `[4]` = 4, to 0
  (Aldric's `[16]`+4 is unneeded). They **bind and gag** him rather than kill,
  honoring the no-bodies brief as far as a roused house allows.
- **Escape:** they leave on foot through the servant gate (the gate, not a second
  wall-climb, is the only reason the armored Aldric gets out). Pip is laden and
  slow, `[7]`, but the gate is open. The house looses the mastiff; the group runs
  for the treeline on the worst-case Dodge, Aldric `[13]`-1 = **12 vs 12, by a
  hair**. The two spell-slept gate guards never stirred; no one was there to
  shake them awake.

## Outcome

**Success, loud.** The party escapes with the **iron coffer unopened**, the
ledger inside it, to crack at the refuge where Pip can take his time. One guard
bound, none dead, but Veydrin now knows thieves came for that safe. Pip ends at
**4/7 HP** (Aldric still holds an unused _Cure Light Wounds_); the rest are
untouched; Mireth is spell-dry.

Every class did a job the others could not: Mireth's _Sleep_ erased the opening
patrol with no save and no noise; Aldric's CHA 16 and his robes talked the party
past the butler; Garren supplied the second body that made two-on-one reliable;
Pip carried the infiltration end to end and improvised past his own cold streak
by stealing the whole box.

## Notes

1. **The Turn plus wandering-monster economy is the heist engine.** Every failed
   pick cost a Turn and rolled the wandering die. The tension came straight out
   of the core loop, with no heist subsystem bolted on. This worked well.

2. **No subdue or nonlethal rule.** The no-bodies brief forced the table to
   improvise knocking the guard out. At 0 HP a monster is simply down and, by
   default, dead; the core has no line for choosing nonlethal. A one-line option,
   declare a blow nonlethal to drop a foe helpless instead of killing, would
   directly support heist, capture, and prisoner play. 1e precedent: subdual
   damage. Logged to the backlog.

3. **The Thief's core job is swingy with no careful-work dial.** Pip failed the
   study lock twice and the strongbox three times at +3. Out of combat, with low
   stakes per attempt, there is no way to trade time for reliability (a
   work-carefully bonus, or taking the middle result when safe and unpressured),
   so a cold streak on the class's signature actions has no counterplay but
   retrying into more wandering rolls. Logged to the backlog.

4. **Backstab is gated by a roll the Thief does not control.** The marquee +4 and
   double damage needs the Thief to be the aware side, and the surprise rules
   handed surprise to the NPC here. Fair and tense, and working as written, but
   worth knowing the payoff can simply not happen.

5. **A single-slot Level 1 caster is a one-trick opener.** _Sleep_ was the best
   card in the deck and then Mireth (3 HP) was a passenger. Authentic to 1e, but
   a heist with one caster leans entirely on opening with the right spell.

6. **_Sleep_ (the spell) states no duration** of its own, only the Asleep
   condition (wakes on damage or a round of shaking). The slept guards were ruled
   to stay down until found, which is old-school but rests on GM fiat. A line on
   spell duration might help.

7. **Armor quietly taxes infiltration.** Aldric's medium armor failed the wall
   climb twice (DEX -1) and only the thief-opened gate let him take part. Not a
   gap, but the climb and stealth math leans against armor, which is the right
   instinct for the genre.

Verdict: the core handles a heist without a dedicated subsystem, and every class
found a real job. The two soft spots specific to infiltration and capture are a
missing subdue rule and the Thief's single-roll swinginess with no patience dial.
