# AX&X Solo Playtest, Session 03

A solo, GM-and-players-both run of the **core rules only** (`src/axx.typ`), under
the current rules with the softened Brush with Death table. This is the follow-up
to Session 02, run with the methodology mistakes from that session corrected:

- **Stats were rolled first, then classes and kindred assigned to fit the
  numbers**, and the pricier-armor classes went to the characters who rolled more
  gold.
- **A replacement would be offered on any death** (none occurred).
- **Monster targeting was played as a neutral GM would**, not always for the kill:
  front line first, no gratuitous focus on downed or helpless characters.

The dungeon and the four encounters are held identical to Sessions 01 and 02, with
the same monster HP (ghoul 10; orcs 9/5/5; skeletons 2/6/5; Warden 40). Only the
party and the live dice differ. Every die was a real shell roll, quoted inline as
`[dice] = total`. This was one continuous delve, four encounters in sequence, no
rest between them.

## The Party

Abilities rolled 3d6 in order, classes and kindred then chosen to fit. Mod table:
3 = -3, 4-5 = -2, 6-8 = -1, 9-12 = 0, 13-15 = +1, 16-17 = +2, 18 = +3.

### Thokk, Half-Orc Barbarian (Neutral)

- Rolls: STR 10 (0), DEX 11 (0), CON 12 (0), INT 3 (-3), WIS 10 (0), CHA 11 (0).
- INT 3 ruled out magic; flat stats and the lowest purse suited a cheap-armor bruiser. Half-Orc for the melee bonus and darkvision.
- HP 10 (max d10 + CON 0). AC 11 (hide). Heavy axe, two hands, 1d10+1 (STR 0 + Half-Orc 1), attack +1. 40 ft on foot. +2 vs magic. Saves all +0.
- Gold 80: hide armor 20, heavy weapon 15, rations 5, four oil 8, rope 1, torches 1.

### Dorna, Dwarf Cleric (Lawful)

- Rolls: STR 6 (-1), DEX 7 (-1), CON 9 (0), INT 8 (-1), WIS 10 (0), CHA 13 (+1).
- Weak physicals and a CHA lead with no mechanical home but a faith class. Armor offsets the poor DEX. Dwarf for the hardy Body save.
- HP 6. AC 14 (medium). Mace 1d6-1 blunt, attack +0. Body +2 (Dwarf), Dodge -1, Will +2 (class). 1 slot: Cure Light Wounds. Turn Undead 1d20+1.
- Gold 130: holy symbol 25, medium armor 75, mace 10, rations 5, rope 1.

### Aldric, Human Fighter (Neutral)

- Rolls: STR 10 (0), DEX 9 (0), CON 15 (+1), INT 11 (0), WIS 7 (-1), CHA 9 (0).
- CON 15 and the richest purse made the AC tank. Human +1 to CON, 15 to 16 (+2), crossing a modifier line and adding HP and a Body save.
- HP 10 (max d8 + CON +2). AC 15 (medium + shield). Sword 1d6, attack +1. Body +4 (CON +2, class +2), Dodge 0, Will -1. Carries a backup hammer from the spike kit for blunt work.
- Gold 170: medium armor 75, shield 10, sword 10, spikes and hammer 3, rations 5, four oil 8, rope 1, torches 1.

### Syln, Elf Magic-User (Neutral)

- Rolls: STR 14 (+1), DEX 11 (0), CON 8 (-1), INT 15 (+1), WIS 9 (0), CHA 11 (0).
- INT 15 was the one roll that makes a real caster, so the arcane slot went here even though STR 14 is wasted on a Mage. Elf to guard the fragile body against Sleep and Charm.
- HP 3 (max d4 + CON -1). AC 10. Dagger 1d4+1, oil flasks. **2 slots** (INT 15 grants the bonus): Magic Missile and Sleep. Immune to Sleep and Charmed, +2 vs magic.
- Gold 120: spellbook 15, dagger 2, four oil 8, rations 5, rope 1, torches 1.

Party HP 29. A two-tank front line (Aldric AC 15, Thokk HP 10 and d10), a healer who
can Turn, and a genuine two-slot caster. **No Thief**: none of the rolls fit one, so
the party has no trap or lock specialist, a real gap if the crawl had demanded it.

## First Encounter: The Ghoul

> Ghoul. HD 2, HP 10, AC 14, Atk +2, claw/claw/bite (1d3 each). Any hit: Body save
> vs 12 or Paralyzed. Mindless.

- **Surprise, 1d6 each: party `[1]`, ghoul `[2]`.** Both roll 1-2, so both are caught off guard, a mutual flinch, and no one acts in the surprise round.
- **Round 1** (init party `[2]` vs ghoul `[4]`, ghoul first). The ghoul tears at Aldric in front (AC 15): claw `[7]`+2 = 9, claw `[10]`+2 = 12, bite `[4]`+2 = 6, all miss. Party: Aldric `[19]` hits for 1, Thokk `[4]` misses, Dorna `[16]` hits for 3, Syln's Magic Missile auto-hits for 3. Ghoul 10 -> 3.
- **Round 2** (init party `[2]` vs ghoul `[3]`, ghoul first). Ghoul on Aldric: two misses and a **bite `[15]`+2 = 17 hit** for 2 (Aldric 10 -> 8), forcing a Body save: Aldric `[13]`+2 = 15 vs 12, **made**, not paralyzed. Party: Aldric and Thokk miss, Dorna hits for 2 (ghoul 3 -> 1), Syln's oil throws a natural `[1]`, a miss.
- **Round 3** (init party `[6]` vs ghoul `[2]`, party first). Aldric misses, then **Thokk's axe `[16]`+1 = 17 hits for 5. Ghoul dead.**

**Outcome: ghoul down in 3 rounds, Aldric took 2 and made his save.** The mutual
flinch denied the ghoul a free round, the AC-15 fighter held the front instead of a
paralyzed body, and straight targeting kept it on the tank. Contrast Session 02,
where the same ghoul paralyzed the front-liner in a free round and nearly killed
him.

## Second Encounter: The Orc Patrol

Three orcs, HP 9/5/5, AC 13, +1 (1d8).

- **Surprise:** neither (party `[5]`, orcs `[6]`). **Reaction, 2d6 + CHA: `[1 6]` + 1 = 8**, wary and open to a deal. The party wants the chamber and holds a trump, so Syln looses Sleep.
- **Sleep affects 2d4 = `[1 3]` = 4 HD.** Three 1-HD orcs, so all three are caught, no save.
- **Initiative is a tie (party `[5]`, orcs `[5]`)**, so the orcs get one simultaneous swing as the spell lands, then drop: Orc A `[15]`+1 = 16 hits Aldric (AC 15) for 6 (8 -> 2), Orc B `[10]` misses, Orc C `[17]`+1 = 18 hits Thokk (AC 11) for 5 (10 -> 5). All three then fall asleep.

The party binds the sleeping orcs as prisoners (Dorna is Lawful; no executing the
helpless) and takes the chamber and its **Coin Cache, 600 GP**. Both front-liners
bind wounds, 1d4 once per day: Aldric `[4]` to 6, Thokk `[3]` to 8.

**Outcome: Sleep ended the fight with no deaths.** This is the exact spot where
Session 02 lost its Fighter, because that party's Magic-User had rolled INT 10 and
carried no Sleep. A real two-slot caster trivializes a group of weak living foes.

## Third Encounter: The Skeleton Guards

Three skeletons, HP 2/6/5, AC 13, +1 (1d6). Mindless, resistant to edged and
piercing, vulnerable to blunt. The party's answers are Dorna's mace, Aldric's
backup hammer, and Syln's oil. Thokk's edged axe is halved here.

- **Surprise:** neither (party `[5]`, skels `[4]`). No ambush, unlike Session 02.
- **Dorna's Turn fails** (`[5]`+1 = 6 vs 11), her once-per-fight attempt spent.
- **Round 1** (init party `[2]` vs skels `[5]`, skels first). S1 `[13]`+1 and S3 `[15]`+1 both hit Thokk for 2 and 6, dropping him (8 -> 0); S2 `[1]` misses Aldric. **Thokk Brush `[3]` = Scarred**, which on the new table snaps him back to full HP (10) and clears any ailment. Party: **Aldric's hammer `[16]`-1 = 15 hits** for blunt (4 x 2) = 8, destroying S2 (6); Dorna and Syln miss.
- **Round 2** (init tie `[3]`/`[3]`). S1 `[19]`+1 = 20 hits Thokk for 5 (10 -> 5), S3 `[5]` misses. Simultaneously Dorna's mace `[15]` kills S1 (2), Aldric's hammer `[15]` hits S3 for 2 (5 -> 3); Thokk and Syln miss.
- **Round 3** (init tie). S3 `[14]`+1 = 15 hits Thokk for 4 (5 -> 1). Dorna, Aldric, Syln all miss; Thokk's axe `[13]` hits but edged-halved to 1 (S3 3 -> 2). Thokk, at 1 HP, falls back.
- **Round 4** (init party `[4]` vs skels `[3]`, party first). **Dorna's mace `[16]` lands a doubled 10, shattering S3.**

**Outcome: skeletons cleared in 4 rounds, no deaths.** It dragged on poor party
accuracy and Thokk's halved axe, and Thokk hit 0 once (Scarred, back to full). The
blunt-weapon puzzle rewarded Dorna, whose mace did the real work. Dorna then spends
her one slot on Cure Light Wounds for Thokk, 1d8 = `[5]`, to 6, keeping Turn Undead
(a separate ability) in reserve.

## Fourth Encounter: The Drowned Warden

The session 01 boss build, held identical: HD 5, HP 40, AC 15, two slams a round at
+5 for 1d8, vulnerable to fire so oil doubles, undead and Mindless but Turnable,
and the telegraphed Undertow (a wave a round after it rears, Dodge vs 15 or 2d6 and
Prone and dragged). The party's two levers are Syln's doubled oil and Dorna's Turn.

- **Surprise:** neither (party `[5]`, warden `[5]`). **Initiative a tie (`[4]`/`[4]`)**, simultaneous rounds, as in Session 01.
- **Dorna's Turn fails** (`[8]`+1 = 9 vs 15). The wildcard is gone.
- **Round 1** (simultaneous). The Warden slams Thokk `[6]`+5 = 11 for 1 (6 -> 5) and **Aldric `[10]`+5 = 15 for 7**, dropping him (6 -> 0). In the same tick Aldric's sword `[18]` still lands for 4 (Warden 40 -> 36); Syln's oil `[4]` and Thokk's axe `[7]` miss. **Aldric Brush `[4]` = Shaken**: 1 HP, drops his sword, Injured.

With the tank at 1 HP and weaponless, the Warden at 36, and the party's damage
sputtering against AC 15, the fight is unwinnable: perhaps 5 to 8 damage on a good
round against two +5 slams a round. The party has 600 GP and four live bodies.
Dorna calls the retreat, which is the judgment Session 02's party failed to make.

- **Retreat round** (init tie, party Runs). The vault-bound Warden gets parting slams on the rearguard Thokk (AC 11): `[5]`+5 = 10 miss, `[19]`+5 = 24 hits for 6, dropping him (5 -> 0). **Thokk Brush**, his second today, so +1: raw `[4]` + 1 = **5 = Grievous Wound**, unconscious, to be carried. The party clears the threshold and the guardian does not follow.

**Outcome: the party withdraws intact.** The Warden is left alive at 36 HP and the
reliquary abandoned. Thokk is unconscious (Grievous), Aldric is at 1 HP and Injured,
Dorna and Syln are unhurt. Nobody died.

## Outcome and Aftermath

- **All four characters alive.** Thokk: Grievous Wound (unconscious, Injured). Aldric: Shaken (1 HP, Injured, lost his sword). Dorna and Syln: unhurt.
- Loot carried out: the **600 GP** orc cache and two or three bound orc prisoners.
- The Drowned Warden survives; the reliquary is not taken.

XP:

- Ghoul: 50. First visit, depth 1: 100. Orcs: 75. Skeletons: 75. Boss: 0 (not defeated).
- Combat and exploration 300, plus 600 gold carried out. About 900 in all once banked, shared among four living characters.

Three runs, same dungeon, same monster HP:

- **Session 01:** all four encounters cleared, no deaths until one maiming in the boss, about 1,400 XP. (Played on the older, looser AC and the pre-reorder Brush table.)
- **Session 02:** two dead, one grievously wounded, routed before the boss, about 225 XP. (Current AC, old 6-is-Slain Brush table, mismatched classes, aggressive GM.)
- **Session 03:** no deaths, one grievous and one injured, a chosen retreat from the boss, about 900 XP and 600 gold. (Current AC, softened Brush table, classes fit to rolls, neutral GM.)

## Playtest Notes

1. **The softened Brush table did its job.** Three characters hit 0 HP and none
   died: Thokk's Scarred snapped him to full, Aldric's Shaken left him at 1, and
   Thokk's second drop was a Grievous Wound, bad but survivable. On the old table,
   two of those drops would have been likely kills, which is exactly what happened
   in Session 02. Removing Slain from the 1d6 range means a first drop can never
   kill outright, which is the whole difference between a battered party and a
   corpse pile.

2. **A real caster neutralizes the weak-group fight entirely.** Syln had two slots
   because he was built to his INT 15, and Sleep ended the orc encounter before it
   could threaten anyone. Session 02's forced INT-10 Magic-User had no such tool
   and lost the Fighter in the same spot. This is the clearest argument yet both
   for rolling before assigning classes and for looking hard at whether a Mage
   should get a second slot without needing a strong INT.

3. **The scaled solo boss is still too much for a sized-to-level party, and that
   reads as correct.** The Warden's 40 HP and two +5 slams against AC 10 to 15,
   facing a party that deals single digits a round, is not a fight this group can
   win. Retreat was the right call, and the game supported it: the party walked
   away with the loot and their lives. The lever remains monster HP, attack count,
   and to-hit versus party AC, not monster count or morale.

4. **Party accuracy against AC 15 is very low.** Attack bonuses of +0 and +1 need a
   14 or 15 on the die against the boss, so most swings miss. Against a 40 HP pool
   that is what makes the fight hopeless. If the boss tier is meant to be beatable
   by a level-1 party, either the party needs more reliable damage or the solo
   wants less HP; if it is meant to be a wall they flee, it works as is.

5. **Retreat is a first-class outcome and should be framed as one.** The difference
   between Session 02 (pressed on, two dead) and Session 03 (withdrew, none dead)
   was a single judgment call. Nothing in the core text tells a new GM that walking
   away with the loot is a win, and it is worth a line somewhere that says so.

6. **Turn Undead as a coin-flip neutralize is swingy.** Dorna's Turn was the
   party's one button against both the skeletons and the boss, and it failed both
   times (needing a 10 then a 14 on the die). When a single roll can erase an
   encounter or do nothing, the fight's shape rides on it. Thematic, but worth
   noting that a level-1 Cleric with WIS 0 turns rarely.

7. **The edged axe was dead weight against the skeletons.** Thokk, the hardest
   hitter, had his damage halved for the whole fight because his only weapon was
   edged. A barbarian with one big weapon has no blunt option, which left him
   swinging for 1s. Worth noting that the damage-type puzzle can sideline a whole
   class if the party's loadout is wrong, which is either good tactical texture or
   a trap for a single-weapon character, depending on intent.

8. **The no-Thief party never paid for it here, but the gap is real.** Rolling
   classes to fit produced no Thief, and the crypt happened not to punish it. A
   dungeon with locked doors or disarmable traps would have, and the stocking table
   can produce trap rooms. Worth remembering that an emergent party can lack a
   whole role.
